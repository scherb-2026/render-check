Render check

Drag across an image or the slider below it to move the split; ← → nudge a focused handle (Shift for bigger steps). Hovering a box: Space blinks to the render only. S starts or stops the sweep, 1–9 switch renders. For videos: K play/pause, , . step one frame. Videos play by themselves while they are in the middle of the window; stop one and it stays stopped until you scroll away and back. Switch datasets with the bar on the far left, and between Videos and Images with the narrow bar beside it; at the bottom of a dataset a button leads to the next one.
